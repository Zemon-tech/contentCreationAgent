# 1. Brand Overview

## What is Rise & Shine?

**Rise & Shine (RAS)** is a high-signal social media content distribution brand for people building the future of technology and business, with an India-first perspective.

RAS covers:

- AI and emerging technology
- Startups and business
- Investments and funding
- New products and companies
- Founder intelligence
- New opportunities
- Indian technology ecosystem
- Global developments relevant to Indian builders
- Research, tools, reports, and ideas worth knowing
- Important news that can affect founders and builders

RAS is **not intended to behave like a traditional news publication**.

Its role is:

> **Find → Filter → Explain → Distribute**

The editorial promise is:

> **What happened. Why it matters.**

# 4. Brand Philosophy

## Signal Over Noise

The internet produces more information than a founder can realistically consume.

RAS exists to reduce that information overload.

The audience should feel:

> **"Someone already did the filtering for me."**

Every important piece of content should ideally answer:

1. **What happened?**
2. **Why does it matter?**
3. **Who should care?**
4. **What should a builder understand from it?**

---

# 5. Editorial Formula

Every RAS story should aim to follow:

> **Signal + Context + Relevance**

### Signal

What actually happened?

### Context

What is the background?

### Relevance

Why should a founder, builder, investor, or technology professional care?

The goal is not simply to distribute information.

The goal is to make the audience **smarter in less time**.

# 15. Colour Usage Rules

## Do

- Use colour to establish hierarchy.
- Keep most surfaces neutral.
- Use Vermilion as the recognisable RAS signal.
- Use category colours consistently.
- Maintain strong contrast.
- Let whitespace breathe.
- Use accent colours to highlight important information.

## Don't

- Use every accent colour in one composition.
- Create rainbow compositions.
- Use neon gradients by default.
- Use purple/cyan "AI gradients."
- Use colour simply for decoration.
- Let accent colours compete with the headline.
- Introduce random colours without adding them to the brand system.

# 17. Editorial Typeface

## Instrument Serif

Instrument Serif is the editorial accent typeface.

Use it selectively to create contrast and editorial personality.

### Example

**THE AI RACE**

*is already moving.*

Use for:

- Editorial statements
- Quotes
- Short emphasis
- Feature stories
- Cover-style headlines

Do not use it for:

- Dense body copy
- Navigation
- Metadata
- Technical information

---

# 18. Typography Principles

### Large

Headlines should be allowed to dominate the composition.

### Tight

Display typography should use compact line-height.

### Short

Use fewer words.

### Hierarchical

Use size and weight before adding additional colours.

### Editorial

Typography should feel composed rather than like a software dashboard.

---

## Avoid

- Too many font families
- Decorative fonts
- Excessively tight body copy
- Tiny unreadable text
- Excessive all-caps
- Artificial luxury-style letter spacing
- Random typography treatments

## 19.1 Primary Logo

The RAS primary logo is a custom geometric **RAS wordmark**.

# 24. Logo Rules

## Do

- Preserve original proportions.
- Maintain clear space.
- Use approved light/dark variants.
- Keep the sunrise element intact.
- Use the standalone symbol at small sizes.

## Don't

- Stretch the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Change the logo proportions.
- Add unnecessary shadows.
- Add glow to the full wordmark.
- Change the sunrise colour arbitrarily.
- Add random gradients to the letterforms.
- Place the logo on noisy imagery without a clear container.

# 26. Layout System

Use an editorial grid.

Preferred layouts include:

- 2-column compositions
- 3-column compositions
- Strong margins
- Asymmetric layouts
- Large whitespace
- Clear information blocks
- Strong alignment
- Deliberate negative space

The grid should create structure without making the content feel like an enterprise dashboard.
