# Higgsfield Cinematography, Optics & Soul Aesthetics

This guide integrates the directing principles of **Higgsfield Cinema Studio 2.0/3.5** and the **Higgsfield Soul Realism Philosophy** into MiniMax H3 scene generation.

---

## 1. The "Hero Frame First" Directing Philosophy

Higgsfield's core breakthrough in professional AI filmmaking is the **"Hero Frame First"** discipline:
1. **Never Animate an Undefined Image**: If the initial frame has ambiguous lighting, loose character features, or floating geometry, the model will hallucinate and distort across the temporal timeline.
2. **Lock Optics & Sensor First**: Before describing movement, lock the physical camera rig, sensor format, lens profile, focal length, and aperture.
3. **Anchor Identity Explicitly**: Describe exact bone structure, facial micro-textures, age, and tactile wardrobe materials. Identity must be an enforced constraint, not an interpretive guess.

## 2. Optical Rig Profiles & Lens Character

Higgsfield Cinema Studio models real-world optical glass characteristics. Use these curated lens profiles:

### 2.1 Cooke S4/i Prime Profile ("The Cooke Look")
- **Character**: Warm, organic, gentle contrast, rounded portrait fall-off, silky circular bokeh.
- **Application**: Narrative drama, luxury lifestyle, character emotional close-ups, intimate dialogue.
- **Prompt Token**: *"Captured on an ARRI Alexa Mini LF paired with a 50mm Cooke S4/i Prime at f/2.0, rendering smooth skin roll-off and painterly circular background bokeh."*

### 2.2 Zeiss Supreme Prime Profile
- **Character**: Razor-sharp modern resolution, clinical optical precision, high micro-contrast, zero chromatic aberration, neutral color fidelity.
- **Application**: Technology commercials, architectural spaces, clean studio product showcases, high-end SaaS campaigns.
- **Prompt Token**: *"Shot on an 8K full-frame sensor through a 35mm Zeiss Supreme Prime at f/4.0, preserving razor-sharp geometric lines and pristine architectural clarity."*

### 2.3 Panavision C-Series Anamorphic (2.39:1)
- **Character**: Horizontal oval bokeh, subtle cyan/blue horizontal flare on specular highlights, barrel distortion at wider angles, shallow vertical depth of field.
- **Application**: Cinematic widescreen blockbusters, night vehicle chases, evocative neon streetscapes.
- **Prompt Token**: *"Captured with Panavision C-Series 2x anamorphic optics at 2.39:1 aspect ratio, showcasing subtle horizontal anamorphic flares and cylindrical bokeh."*

### 2.4 Vintage Super 16mm / Angénieux Zoom
- **Character**: Textured organic grain, gentle halation around highlights, warm amber flare, tactile micro-abrasions.
- **Application**: Arthouse indie films, documentary flashbacks, nostalgic fashion editorials.

## 4. Higgsfield Signature Camera Dynamics

Combine Higgsfield dynamic moves with MiniMax H3's **Motion Type + Amplitude + Speed** grammar:

### 4.1 The Dolly Zoom ("Vertigo Effect")
- **Physics**: The camera physically dollies forward while the lens zooms out simultaneously (or vice-versa), keeping the subject the exact same size while the background radically warps in perspective.
- **Directing Formula**:
  `The camera pushes in with large amplitude at fast speed while optical focal length zooms out, creating a striking Hitchcock vertigo perspective warp behind the subject.`

### 4.2 Snorricam (Body-Mounted Actor Rig)
- **Physics**: The camera is clamped to the actor's chest or waist, facing directly at their face. The subject remains dead-centered and static in frame while the world swings chaotically behind them.
- **Directing Formula**:
  `A locked-off Snorricam rig holds dead-center focus on the runner's face; her head remains static in the composition while the blurred urban background bobs and rushes past.`

### 4.3 FPV Drone Fly-Through / Dive
- **Physics**: Smooth 3-axis kinetic banking, diving from high elevation through narrow spaces with smooth roll and level recovery.
- **Directing Formula**:
  `The camera dives forward along a dynamic FPV flight path at fast speed, banking smoothly into a left roll through the archway before leveling out.`

### 4.4 Orbit / Bullet Time 360
- **Physics**: Camera travels along a continuous circular arc around the subject.
- **Directing Formula**:
  `The camera completes a 180-degree arc shot with large amplitude at moderate speed around the speaker, keeping tack-sharp eye focus as background lighting shifts.`

### 4.5 Kinetic Speed Ramping
- **Physics**: Transitioning seamlessly from standard real-time motion to high-frame-rate slow-motion and snapping back.
- **Directing Formula**:
  `The runner sprints at real-time speed before the motion ramps into smooth 120fps slow-motion as water droplets hang mid-air, snapping cleanly back to normal speed upon foot impact.`

## 5. Higgsfield Soul Realism: Eliminating AI Slop

The "Soul" philosophy demands real human presence and anti-plastic constraints:

1. **Micro-Expressions & Biomechanics**:
   - *Forbidden*: Static smiles, mannequin blank stares, unnatural continuous blinking.
   - *Required*: Involuntary micro-swallows, subtle nostril flaring on exhalation, lip moisture parting, eye micro-saccades reading text.
2. **Tactile Micro-Textures**:
   - *Required*: Natural skin pore structure, faint uneven stubble, peach fuzz along jawline, visible fabric weave (linen slub, raw wool fibers, selvedge denim), authentic dust motes caught in sunbeams.
3. **Motivated Lighting Ratios**:
   - *Forbidden*: Flat broadcast television lighting, dual colored rim-lights with no physical source.
   - *Required*: Motivated key lighting with at least a 2:1 or 3:1 contrast ratio between key and fill, preserving rich natural shadow detail.
