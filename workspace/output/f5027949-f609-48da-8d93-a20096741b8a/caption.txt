DeepSeek-V3 is now available on Hugging Face. The model contains 671 billion parameters, but its mixture-of-experts architecture activates only a subset for any given task. This design allows for efficient inference while maintaining high performance on complex benchmarks. Early evaluations indicate it outperforms leading open-source models, including Llama 3.1-405B. The release continues a trend of narrowing the gap between open and closed-source AI systems, with implications for the future development of artificial general intelligence.
