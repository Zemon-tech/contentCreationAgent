# Commercial Ad Scripting & Narrative Frameworks

## Overview
This reference outlines the world's most effective commercial advertising frameworks, drawing direct inspiration from iconic campaigns by **Apple, Perplexity, OpenAI, Uber, and KeilHQ**. It provides the strategic blueprint for transforming any product, audience, and use case into an unforgettable scene-by-scene video script.

---

## 1. The 5 Iconic Campaign Archetypes

### Archetype 1: The Apple Paradigm — "Grounded Human Craft & Inevitability"
*Inspired by: "The Underdogs", "Behind the Mac", "Think Different"*

- **Core Psychology**: Never make a sterile corporate tech demo. People don't care about software features; they care about **human agency, friction, and overcoming chaotic stakes**.
- **Structural Blueprint**:
  1. **Relatable Chaos (0–8s)**: A high-stakes deadline, miscommunication, or logistical tangle that everyone recognizes. The characters are messy, stressed, but genuine.
  2. **Product as the Calm Catalyst (8–20s)**: The product is not introduced with trumpets; it is quietly opened, tapped, or leveraged as a natural extension of human hands.
  3. **Kinetic Momentum (20–26s)**: Fast, rhythmic editing where actions align with snappy sound effects (clicks, taps, rapid footsteps). The problem dissolves into flow.
  4. **Quiet Human Payoff (26–30s)**: The victory is personal, humorous, or intimate. The Apple logo appears only at the final second with restrained sound.
- **Tone**: Kinetic, witty, cinematic realism, tactile foley, natural dialogue over voiceover.

---

### Archetype 2: The Perplexity Paradigm — "The Radical Friction Contrast"
*Inspired by: "Why Search When You Can Know?", "Where Curiosity Begins"*

- **Core Psychology**: Expose the absurd pain of the legacy status quo. Viewers tolerate cumbersome, noisy workflows until you vividly contrast them with instantaneous, clean clarity.
- **Structural Blueprint**:
  1. **The Friction Trap (0–6s)**: Show the exasperating modern reality: 15 browser tabs, paywalls, spammy SEO articles, blinking ad banners, mental exhaustion.
  2. **The Breaking Point (6–10s)**: The protagonist pauses, visibly fatigued by the noise. A bold visual pattern interrupt occurs.
  3. **The Single Question (10–18s)**: A clean, conversational question is posed. The screen purges all clutter.
  4. **Instant Synthesized Truth (18–25s)**: Clear, sourced, synthesized understanding flows effortlessly. The protagonist's face shifts from tension to enlightened calm.
  5. **The Provocative Question CTA (25–30s)**: *"Why search when you can know? Perplexity."*
- **Tone**: Intellectual, sharp, high-contrast, visually minimal, provocative pacing.

---

### Archetype 3: The OpenAI Paradigm — "The Poetic Awe Manifesto"
*Inspired by: Sora Launch Films, ChatGPT "Keep Exploring", "New Horizons"*

- **Core Psychology**: Elevate the conversation from utility to **human potential, imagination, and existential wonder**. Connect everyday creation to the grand tapestry of human thought.
- **Structural Blueprint**:
  1. **The Spark of Thought (0–10s)**: A lone creator, researcher, or child staring into space. A philosophical voiceover begins, quiet and intimate.
  2. **Visual Metaphor of Becoming (10–25s)**: Abstract ideas materialize physically on screen. Surreal yet photorealistic transformations (a sketch turning into a living forest, a mathematical equation rippling across water).
  3. **The Expansion (25–45s)**: A montage of human endeavor across disciplines (art, science, architecture, everyday family life) augmented by intelligence.
  4. **The Quiet Reverence Payoff (45–60s)**: The camera settles on a breathtaking, grounded human moment.
  5. **Minimalist Sign-off**: Clean typographic mark, quiet ambient chime.
- **Tone**: Expansive, orchestral, reverent, deep voiceover cadence, large-format cinema optics.

---

### Archetype 4: The Uber / Uber Eats Paradigm — "Relatable Absurdity & Honest Truth"
*Inspired by: "Get Almost, Almost Anything", "Don't Forget"*

- **Core Psychology**: Win user trust through self-deprecating honesty and humorous literalism. Acknowledge what the product *can't* do to make what it *can* do feel miraculous.
- **Structural Blueprint**:
  1. **The Absurd Misinterpretation (0–6s)**: A character takes a phrase literally or attempts to order an impossible human need (e.g. "time to think", "a date with an 1800s duke").
  2. **The Escalate & Subvert (6–18s)**: The scenario plays out with full cinematic conviction, escalating to comic awkwardness.
  3. **The Grounded Reset (18–25s)**: A clean cut back to reality. The actual package or ride arrives right on time with effortless simplicity.
  4. **The Honest Tagline (25–30s)**: *"Get Almost, Almost Anything. Uber Eats."*
- **Tone**: Deadpan, witty, warm, conversational comedic timing, high celebrity or relatable actor charm.

---

### Archetype 5: The KeilHQ Paradigm — "Work Ends. Life Continues."
*Inspired by: Anti-grind B2B productivity commercials*

- **Core Psychology**: Modern knowledge workers are burned out by tools that promise "10x productivity" because they just lead to 10x more emails. The ultimate luxury is not doing more work—it is **getting your life, peace, and family back**.
- **Structural Blueprint**:
  1. **The Heavy Mindspace (0–8s)**: Work competing with life. The laptop is open, notifications buzz, the mind is elsewhere.
  2. **The Frictionless Resolution (8–18s)**: Instead of a frantic dashboard montage, the system silently handles the complexity.
  3. **The Physical Leaving Behind (18–26s)**: The laptop closes. The character stands and physically walks away from the desk into sunlight, dinner, or their child's world.
  4. **The Human Landing (26–30s)**: Quiet confidence. *"Your life shouldn't have to feel like work. KeilHQ."*
- **Tone**: Candid editorial lifestyle, warm Kodak Portra 400 aesthetic, soft domestic lighting, quiet emotional weight.

## 2. The Universal "Hook, Hold, Payoff" Timing Engine

Every commercial script must be mathematically budgeted to ensure viewer retention across paid social (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, TV):

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 00:00 - 00:03 │ THE HOOK: Visual Interrupt & Core Stake│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 00:03 - 00:15 │ THE HOLD: Agitation & Product Catalyst │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 00:15 - 00:24 │ THE FLOW: Breakthrough & Transformation│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 00:24 - 00:30 │ THE PAYOFF: Emotional Landing & CTA    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Timecode | Story Beat | Director's Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00:00 - 00:03** | **The Hook** | Stop the scroll. Zero logos. Start in media res with high visual tension or intriguing physical action. |
| **00:03 - 00:10** | **The Friction / Agitation** | Clarify the stakes. Show the relatable villain (clutter, delay, manual toil, cognitive overload). |
| **00:10 - 00:18** | **The Pivot / Catalyst** | The product enters seamlessly. Focus on the *action of the user*, not a boring software tutorial. |
| **00:18 - 00:25** | **The Flow State** | The visual and auditory world relaxes. Camera moves become fluid, lighting warms, pacing settles. |
| **00:25 - 00:30** | **The Payoff & Brand Landing** | Emotional resolution. Physical props anchored. Clear, minimal typography and CTA. |

## 3. Product Discovery & Audience Profiling Framework

Before writing a single scene, answer these four core strategic questions:

1. **The True Villain**: What is the unseen emotional or physical enemy? (e.g., Not "inefficient spreadsheets", but *"dread on Sunday night"*).
2. **The Target Hero**: Who is the protagonist? What are their daily physical micro-habits? What clothes do they wear? Where do they work?
3. **The Magic Moment ("Aha!")**: What exact physical moment demonstrates the product's genius? (e.g., The closing of the laptop, the single sentence typed, the sigh of relief).
4. **The Emotional Destination**: How must the viewer feel when the screen fades to black? (e.g., Empowered, relieved, amused, awed, hungry for clarity).
