# House Style — Contemporary Neoclassical Editorial Collage (normative)

Style: Contemporary Neoclassical Editorial Collage. Archetype: `neoclassical-pop-editorial`.
Aliases: surreal editorial collage, vintage engraving modernism, classical-meets-future,
neoclassical pop art, editorial print collage, antique illustration + contemporary symbolism.

## Core tension: OLD × NEW
Historical: Greco-Roman sculpture, marble, classical architecture, antique/copperplate
engraving, botanical/Victorian scientific illustration, Renaissance refs, classical
landscape/portraiture, old diagrams, antique objects. Expressed via technique, architecture,
object, pose, costume, composition, symbolism — NOT always a Greek statue.
Contemporary: laptops, phones, AI, software, data, robots, cloud, office, social, money,
satellites, networks, transport, products, abstract tech. Unexpected but coherent collision.

## Medium (mixed-media editorial collage)
Antique engraving, copperplate etching, woodcut, scientific illustration, stipple,
cross-hatched ink, vintage print, halftone, screen print, risograph, paper collage.
NEVER: generic digital/vector, glossy CGI, stock photo, generic cinematic concept art.

## Hero: person/statue/object/machine/building/animal/symbolic hybrid; usually 50–80% (flexible).

## Color (intentional, never all at once)
House: #087FEA electric blue, #0B0C0C deep ink, #F3EFE5 warm paper, #F4E900 acid yellow,
#18A8C7 cyan; optional #FF8A00 #6A39D7 #C4CF36 #D96532 #253F25.
Hierarchy: SATURATED BACKGROUND + BLACK/CREAM HERO + ONE HIGH-SAT ACCENT.
Hero mostly black/cream/gray/muted historic; environment carries contemporary saturation.

## Texture (non-negotiable)
Cross-hatching, engraving lines, stipple, halftone, paper grain/tooth, ink breakup,
misregistration, risograph/xerox distress, fiber, absorption. Must feel printed.

## Materials
Marble, aged paper, engraved metal, carved stone, oxidized bronze, parchment, wood,
canvas, ink, printed paper, brushed metal, glass. Only when physically meaningful.

## Light / optics / finish / mood
Graphic directional light, ink shadow masses, paper-white highlights, symbolic glow
(~5000–5600K day, 4200–5000K dark — only when useful). Deep apparent focus; no auto
85mm/f1.4/bokeh. Finish: "antique print reproduced through modern editorial screen
printing". Mood: curious, surreal, playful, sophisticated, provocative, strange,
delightful, slightly absurd, editorial, literate. Never generic corporate/epic/sci-fi.

## Non-negotiables
1 old×new tension · 2 strong metaphor · 3 engraved tactile rendering · 4 saturated
contemporary color · 5 black/cream anchor · 6 editorial composition · 7 print texture.
Consistency = grammar (CLASSICAL+CONTEMPORARY+SURREAL+PRINTED+SATURATED+EDITORIAL),
not repeated objects.
