# The 5 Signature Commercial Aesthetics (Reverse-Engineered for LTX-2.3)

## Overview
This reference decodes the 5 signature visual styles seen in top-tier campaigns (ChatGPT by Miles Jay/SMUGGLER, Heinz Wimbledon, Uber Eats "Busy'd Enough", and contemporary high-end lifestyle ads). Each profile translates optics, lighting ratios, Kelvin temperatures, film stocks, and color science into exact parameters for LTX-2.3 generation.

---

## 1. Style 01: "Domestic Golden Slat" (ChatGPT Kitchen / Morning Flow)
*Reference: ChatGPT "The Kitchen Flow" with bandana & steaming pot*

- **Visual DNA**: Warm, lived-in European artisanal domesticity. Direct morning sun sliced into geometric blind slats; warm terracotta and honey pine wood glow.
- **Optics & Framing**:
  - *Lens*: 35mm documentary cinema prime (Cooke S4 or Zeiss Super Speed).
  - *Angle*: Eye-level medium interior wide ($f/4.0$), sharp cookware and midground with soft garden foliage.
  - *Distortion*: Neutral human eye perspective, zero barrel warp.
- **Lighting Architecture**:
  - *Key Light*: Direct 3200K–3500K morning sun entering through window blinds at a 45° angle, projecting sharp linear shadow stripes on walls and tiles.
  - *Fill & Bounce*: Soft warm ambient bounce off terracotta tiles and stainless steel cookware (2.5:1 ratio).
  - *Highlights*: Natural halation and bloom glowing around window frame edges and metallic rims.
- **Film Stock & LUT**:
  - *Emulation*: **Kodak Portra 400** warm analog response.
  - *Contrast*: Lifted, breathable shadows (warm sepia toe), soft S-curve highlight roll-off.
  - *Color Separation*: Terracotta ceramic (`#B86B43`) and honey wood balanced against a saturated chromatic pop (coral phone case `#E85A1E` or fresh green herbs).
- **Physical Textures**:
  - Glazed tile grout, coarse cotton bandana weave, rising steam wisps, polished stainless steel cookware, natural skin pores.

## 2. Style 02: "Moody Transit Intimacy" (ChatGPT Public Bus / Late Afternoon)
*Reference: ChatGPT "Banter on the Bus" with braided hair & phone*

- **Visual DNA**: Intimate, vulnerable, unforced human connection framed inside urban mass transit. Deep indigo textured shadows contrasted with luminous, warm skin highlights.
- **Optics & Framing**:
  - *Lens*: 24mm–28mm wide cinema prime.
  - *Angle*: Seated eye-level two-shot framed symmetrically between high transit seat headrests.
  - *Distortion*: Gentle wide-angle environmental expansion emphasizing the proximity of the two friends.
  - *Depth of Field*: Shallow ($f/2.2$–$f/2.8$). Foreground fabric softens; hands, phone, and facial smiles are tack-sharp.
- **Lighting Architecture**:
  - *Key Light*: Diffuse 5000K afternoon daylight entering through side bus windows, grazing braided hair and cheekbones.
  - *Fill*: Natural ambient vehicle occlusion, falling into moody deep shadows toward the floor (3:1 contrast ratio).
  - *Temperature*: Cool blue-cyan ambient fabric interior contrasted with warm, saturated honey skin tones.
- **Film Stock & LUT**:
  - *Emulation*: **Kodak Vision3 500T (5219)** 35mm film stock.
  - *Grade*: Subtle cyan-teal shadow undertone with rich, uncompressed amber skin highlights and fine organic 35mm grain.
- **Physical Textures**:
  - Coarse patterned transit moquette velour, braided scalp geometry, smooth matte smartphone case, polished steel handrails.

## 3. Style 03: "Quiet Athletic Sanctuary" (ChatGPT High-Ceiling Workout)
*Reference: ChatGPT "Home Workout Reset" with dumbbells & orange tee*

- **Visual DNA**: Solitary discipline, mental endurance, and quiet internal focus within a stately residential room. Deep earth tones with a single, highly saturated chromatic anchor.
- **Optics & Framing**:
  - *Lens*: 28mm–32mm wide prime lens.
  - *Angle*: Slightly elevated (1.5m height), looking down toward the seated athlete at 0.5m bench level.
  - *Framing*: Environmental wide full-body composition.
  - *Depth of Field*: Deep focus ($f/5.6$), keeping footwear, chrome weights, and background leather sofa readable.
- **Lighting Architecture**:
  - *Key Light*: Diffuse 5000K daylight filtered through white linen window drapes on frame left.
  - *Fill*: Soft, warm bounce off mustard walls and parquet/carpet flooring (2.5:1 ratio).
  - *Specular*: Clean specular highlights glinting off knurled chrome dumbbell plates.
- **Film Stock & LUT**:
  - *Emulation*: **Kodak Tri-X / Portra hybrid** aesthetic.
  - *Grade*: Earthy desaturation (cognac leather, mustard plaster) energized by a single ultra-saturated focal color (vibrant gym orange `#E85A1E`).
- **Physical Textures**:
  - Knurled steel handles, plush wool carpet pile, cracked vintage leather sofa, sheer linen weave, athletic sweat sheen.

## 4. Style 04: "Subdued English Crowd & Focal Pop" (Heinz Wimbledon Bleachers)
*Reference: Heinz "It Has to Be Heinz" hot dog squeeze*

- **Visual DNA**: Playful human obsession amidst restrained British summer crowd decorum. Telephoto background compression, muted neutral spectators, and a razor-sharp saturated condiment pop.
- **Optics & Framing**:
  - *Lens*: 85mm–105mm medium telephoto.
  - *Optics*: Heavy spatial compression, zero perspective distortion, flat authentic facial proportions.
  - *Framing*: Medium crowd shot (subject waist-up), subject anchored dead-center.
  - *Depth of Field*: Moderate ($f/4.0$). Subject and bottle are razor sharp; adjacent spectators soften gently.
- **Lighting Architecture**:
  - *Key Light*: Diffuse 5400K high-altitude overcast open-sky daylight at ~65°.
  - *Lighting Quality*: Wrap-around, soft, non-directional illumination with 1.8:1 gentle contrast ratio.
  - *Shadows*: Clean, lifted shadows beneath cap visors and collars without dark crushing.
- **Film Stock & LUT**:
  - *Emulation*: **Kodak Vision3 250D (5207)** Daylight stock.
  - *Grade*: Kodak 2383 Print LUT with muted summer pastels (sand, ecru, washed denim) and isolated hyper-saturated red (`#D91414`) on the Heinz label and bottle.
- **Physical Textures**:
  - Ribbed knit wool, cotton polo, corduroy cap, plastic condiment squeeze bottle, crusty bread crumb, soft cotton visor.

## 5. Style 05: "Urban Pastoral Whimsy" (Uber Eats Rooftop Beekeeper)
*Reference: Uber Eats "When You've Busy'd Enough" rooftop apiary*

- **Visual DNA**: Eccentric personal devotion rewarded with effortless comfort. Symmetrical environmental wide portrait set against weathered rooftop textures.
- **Optics & Framing**:
  - *Lens*: 35mm cinema prime.
  - *Angle*: Eye-level, perfectly level horizon, centered symmetrical composition.
  - *Framing*: Environmental wide portrait balancing human subject, rustic beehives, and distant London chimneys.
  - *Depth of Field*: Moderate ($f/4.5$), rendering the wooden beehives, beekeeper, and foreground delivery bag in crisp clarity.
- **Lighting Architecture**:
  - *Key Light*: Omnidirectional 5600K soft British overcast sky.
  - *Lighting Quality*: Flat, non-harsh ambient wrap with subtle directional falloff from frame left.
  - *Contrast*: Low commercial contrast (1.5:1), fully open shadows under beehives and planters.
- **Film Stock & LUT**:
  - *Emulation*: **Kodak Vision3 250D Daylight** with clean commercial saturation.
  - *Grade*: Natural earthy warmth (weathered wood, terracotta clay, aged brick) punctuated by vivid brand Kelly Green (`#06C167`).
- **Physical Textures**:
  - Heavy canvas beekeeper suit, wire mesh veil, weathered splintered wood, terracotta pots, fresh burger bun, brass smoker.
